We add a direct delay ablation in the subsection "Ablation of the delay" and its table. We fix tau at zero and retrain all remaining parameters. The cohort transfer becomes Q_0(t)=p(t)I(t), so the pathway remains active without a waiting time. The attention window remains unchanged. Both variants use the same training, validation, and test splits, four events, three folds, and three seeds. We retrain both variants for a total of 72 fits with the same maximum budget of 260 epochs. Validation alone selects checkpoints and deployment seeds. The selected test NRMSE is 0.436389 for the full model and 0.436566 for tau=0. The difference (tau=0 minus full) is +0.000178, with a paired 95% bootstrap interval of [-0.000053, 0.000557]. The interval includes zero, so this experiment does not establish a clear forecasting benefit from a positive delay. The table also reports variation across seeds, NMAE, and runtime. This added experiment concerns forecasting on the observed events. It does not supply a separate synthetic parameter recovery or noise and sparsity study for the delay ablation.
